# Glossary: sections and qualifiers

Counts for any section or qualifier are in `tables/` (cube, tables B, C, C2). The 2025 law text is not in this folder; what is
said about it here is from a reading of IC 8-1-26 (2025 code year).

## Evidence tags (read before quoting anything below)
- **LAW**: the 2025 law text says it (section heading and subsection paraphrased).
- **INFERRED**: my matching of an order cite to a 2025 subsection, by content, penalty amount or timing. Say "appears to".
- **LABEL ONLY**: the orders' own word is the only evidence. Do not state a legal meaning.

## Letters in the orders versus the 2025 text (INFERRED)
The 2025 text places the penalty for failing to give notice at 16(i) and the operator's pipeline penalty at 18(j).
The orders print 16(h) and 18(h) for damages up to 2024 and print 16(i) and 18(j) only for damages dated 2025 (table B3 in
`tables/01_overview_time.md` gives the first order date and the damage years of each). The one exception is a single 18(j) case
with a 2021 damage date (case 25638, order 2022-05-18; an excavator respondent, a $10,000 recommendation, and a note about video of
the excavator changing the markings); it matches the 2025 18(l) (knowingly moving or altering locate markings, ceiling $10,000).
Reading: 16(h) and 16(i) (and 18(h) and 18(j)) are the same duty under older and newer subsection letters. Merge them only when asked,
and then say so. Report by base section as printed otherwise.

## Qualifiers (a word in parentheses after the cite)
The orders add these words. The 2025 law does not define any of them. Apart from the one noted, the only evidence is the label.
Which base sections carry which qualifier: table C2.
| Qualifier | Seen with | Tag | Plain reading to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| (expire) | 16(h), 16(i), one 20(a)(2) | LABEL ONLY, with a LAW hint | The 2025 16(g) says a notice expires after 20 days. The label fits "the locate request had expired", but the orders do not say so. Write "the orders' (expire) label". |
| (prior) | 16(h), 16(i) | LABEL ONLY | No meaning given. Do not expand it. |
| (out) | 16(h), 16(i) | LABEL ONLY | No meaning given. Do not expand it. |
| (locator-error) | 18(h), 18(j) | LABEL ONLY | Name suggests the locate marking was wrong or missing; the orders give no text. |
| (records) | 18(h), 18(j) | LABEL ONLY | Name suggests a records problem; the orders give no text. |
| (no-response) | 18(h), 18(j) | LABEL ONLY | Name suggests no positive response was given (compare 2025 18(c)); the orders give no text. |
| (other) | 16(h), 16(i) | LABEL ONLY | A catch-all label. |

A row with no qualifier is its own group ('-' in the tables). A parenthesized single letter or digit, such as (h), (a)(2), (3)(b), is
part of the section, not a qualifier (PREAMBLE).

## Recommendation wording (what the penalty text looks like)
Table R in `tables/03_penalty_stats.md` lists every text with 3 or more cases. The committee recommends a civil penalty, a warning letter,
or training (with a penalty if the respondent does not complete it); the 2025 law lists those options at 23(h). Escalation wording
("no ticket escalation - additional $1,000", "concealment", "self-repair") adds to the first amount and the headline ignores it.
"Special instructions: ..." after a warning letter is a note on the letter (table R2). Who may be recommended what, and the
first-time homeowner limit, are in 23(i) and 23(j) of the law, which this folder does not contain.
