## Answer Q7-02

Mean headline penalty per non-utility-respondent case, including $0 cases:

- **2021:** **$2,375**. That is $2,912,200 over 1,226 cases, or $2,375.37 before rounding.
- **2025:** **$3,041**. That is $3,731,000 over 1,227 cases, or $3,040.75 before rounding.

I got both from the folder's tool: `python3 reference/tools/cases.py stat --field headline --utility no --year 2021` (and the same with `--year 2025`). Year here is the order year, and each case is one row.

- **Zero-dollar cases:** The means include them. There are 503 in 2021 and 234 in 2025.
- **Training wording:** Many recommendations read "Training or $5,000 if respondent fails to complete training". Under the headline definition these count as $5,000 even though completing the training avoids the penalty. I did not exclude them.
- **Penalty, not payment:** These are recommended penalties. The data doesn't show whether anyone paid.

## Answer Q7-03

Mean headline penalty per non-utility-respondent case, with $0 cases included:

| Year | Non-utility cases | Total headline penalties | Mean per case |

| 2025 | 1,227 | $3,731,000 | **$3,041** |

The 2021 mean is $2,375.37 and the 2025 mean is $3,040.75, rounded to the nearest dollar.

- **Years:** I took each order's year from the date in its file name, such as `ord_PSD_110321`. The order text itself shows no date.
- **Blank pages:** I dropped four blank template pages. They have no respondent or case.
- **Scanned order (2021-11-03):** I read the text layer as scanned, and the OCR looked clean enough that no utility names were misspelled.
- **Checkbox forms (2021-04-21):** Eight older-style forms in this order have an OCR-garbled checked box, so I read the box by eye. Seven were marked "Training or $5,000 civil penalty", which I counted as $5,000. One, Pioneer Masonry, was marked "Civil penalty of $1,500", which I counted as $1,500. These choices are the only judgment calls in the totals. If the OCR misread a box, the 2021 mean would shift slightly.
- **Warning-letter cases:** Cases with only a warning letter count as $0. That is 233 cases in 2025 and about 500 in 2021.
- **Printed-amount quirks:** "$3, 500" counts as $3,500. A case recommending "$10,000; training or $5,000…" counts as $10,000.
