## How answers are scored

Each question has two or more required numbers. Per number: within tolerance = full, within the wider band
= half, otherwise zero. A question scores 2 (all numbers full), 1 (at least half the credit) or 0.
Percentages: full within ±0.5 points, half within ±2 points. Counts: full within ±1%, half within ±5%.
Dollar amounts: full if exact, half within ±10%. A named answer (a year, a company) must match exactly.
An answer labelled "estimate" is scored the same way: no credit for refusing, no penalty for saying so.

- **Unreadable cites:** six forms have a blank cite in the extracted text, and I had no PDF tool to read the images. They are not in either mean. One of them, case 24409, has a $5,000 recommendation. The other five have no recommendation text.
- **Nunc pro tunc order:** the file `ord_PSDNPT_040622` is a correction order that repeats case 24633, an 18(h) case. It does not affect either mean.

## Answer Q3-06

Among cases with a headline penalty above $0, the mean headline penalty is **$4,199** for base section 16(h) and **$3,787** for base section 20(a)(2).

| Base section | Cases with headline > $0 | Sum of headline penalties | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|

- `python3 reference/tools/cases.py stat --base "16(h)" --penalty-min 1 --field headline --receipts 2`

Example cases: 16(h) has ord_PSD_032122.pdf p120 case 24846 and ord_PSD_051822.pdf p7 case 25064. 20(a)(2) has ord_PSD_020823.pdf p2 case 21726 and ord_PSD_101123.pdf p7 case 29226.
