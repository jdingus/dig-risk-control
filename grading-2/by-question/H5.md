## Answer H5-01

Both measures moved the same way from 2023 to 2024. The share of non-utility cases with a headline penalty of at least $5,000 rose, and the utility share of all cases fell. All figures count rows (one row is one case) by order year, and I computed them with `reference/tools/cases.py`.

**(a) Non-utility respondent cases with a headline penalty of at least $5,000**

| Year | Cases | Share |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 623 of 1,502 | **41.5%** |
| 2024 | 687 of 1,421 | **48.3%** |

**(b) Utility respondent cases as a share of all cases**

| Year | Cases | Share |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 605 of 2,107 | **28.7%** |
| 2024 | 476 of 1,897 | **25.1%** |

The denominators include $0 cases. I took "at least $5,000" literally, so $5,000 counts. I did not run the exclusive (over $5,000) reading.

Each count came from commands of this form, run from the `folder` directory:
- `python3 reference/tools/cases.py count --year 2023 --utility no --penalty-min 5000`
- `python3 reference/tools/cases.py count --year 2023 --utility yes`

The same commands with `--year 2024` give the 2024 figures. I did not pull example case receipts.

These are cases approved in each order year. They do not show a change in dig damage itself.
