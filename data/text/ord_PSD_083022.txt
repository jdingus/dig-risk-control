ORDER OF THE COMMISSION 
 
 Ind. Code ch . 8-1-26 provides t he procedure for the Indiana Utility Regulatory 
Commission’s Pipeline Safety Division (“Division”) to find violations of said chapter. Upon 
review of those findings, the Indiana Underground Plant Protection Advisory Committee 
(“Advisory Committee”) reviews those recommendations and recommends civil penalties in the 
Case.   
 
The Cases received pursuant to Ind . Code ch. 8-1-26 and ripe for Commission action are 
attached hereto and collectively referred to as the Advisory Committee Recommendations.  
 
Pursuant to statute, the Advisory Committee has submitted their recommendations to the 
Commission. The Commission UPHOLDS the findings of violations by the Pipeline Safety 
Division and APPROVES the recommendations of the Advisory Committee as listed on the 
attached pages. The Assistant General Counsel to the Division is directed to prepare and send 
notice to the parties advising them of the determination. 
 
Any approved civil penalties are due within 90 days of the date of this Order unless 
payment arrangements are made with the Secretary of the Commission.   
 
Any approved trainings must be completed no later than 180 days from the date of this 
Order or the alternative civil penalty noted in the attached recommendation is due.   
 
IT IS SO ORDERED. 
 
HUSTON, FREEMAN, KREVDA, AND ZIEGNER CONCURE:

and correct copy of the Order as approved.
