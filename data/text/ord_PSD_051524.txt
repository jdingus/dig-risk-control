ORDER OF THE COMMISSION 
 
  Pursuant to Indiana Code chapter 8 -1-26, the Pipeline Safety Division (“Division”) of 
the Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission  (“Commission”) investigates excavation damage 
cases and makes findings violations of said chapter. Upon review of those findings, the Indiana 
Underground Plant Protection Advisory Committee (“Advisory Committee”) reviews those 
findings and recommends civil penalties in each case.   
 
Attached to this order are the cases received , with Division findings of violations and 
Advisory Committee recommendations, that are ripe for Commission action.   
 
The Commission hereby UPHOLDS the findings of violations by the Division and 
APPROVES the recommendations of the Advisory Committee as listed on the attached pages. 
The Office of General Counsel is directed to prepare and send notice to the parties advising them 
of the determination. 
 
Civil penalties approved by the Commission are due within 90 days of the date of this 
Order unless payment arrangements are made with the Commission’s Office of General Counsel.   
 
Trainings approved by the Commission must be completed no later than 90 days from the 
date of this Order unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Commission’s Office of General 
Counsel for good cause shown. Failure to complete the training results in the alternative civil 
penalty noted in the attached recommendation becoming due.   
 
IT IS SO ORDERED. 
 
HUSTON, BENNETT, FREEMAN, AND ZIEGNER CONCUR; VELETA ABSENT:
