- **Non-utility-respondent cases:** 2,039 of 7,249 do, so **28.1%**.

- **Checkbox template:** 7 forms show "D Warning letter" as a checkbox-style option. I counted them because the literal words are present. If those boxes were unchecked, the non-utility figure would drop slightly.
- **Utility count:** the single utility-respondent match is ord_PSD_113022, respondent "Citizens Energy Group – Steam".
