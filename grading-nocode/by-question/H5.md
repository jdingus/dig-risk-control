# H5

**Question:** 2023 to 2024 shift: (a) among non-utility respondent cases, what percentage have a headline penalty of at least $5,000 in 2023 and in 2024; and (b) what percentage of all cases are utility respondent cases in 2023 and in 2024? Denominators include $0 cases. Round each percentage to one decimal.

**Expected answer (from the committed key):** percent_nonutility_penalty_5000plus_2023 = 41.5; percent_nonutility_penalty_5000plus_2024 = 48.3; percent_utility_respondent_cases_2023 = 28.7; percent_utility_respondent_cases_2024 = 25.1

Percentages: full within ±0.5 points, half within ±2 points. Counts: full within ±1%, half within ±5%.
Dollar amounts: full if exact, half within ±10%. A named answer (a year, a company) must match exactly.
An answer labelled "estimate" is scored the same way: no credit for refusing, no penalty for saying so.

## Answer H5-01

**Answer:** (a) Non-utility cases with headline penalty ≥ $5,000: **41.4%** in 2023 (622 of 1,502) and **48.3%** in 2024 (687 of 1,421). (b) Utility-respondent share of all cases: **28.7%** in 2023 (605 of 2,107) and **25.1%** in 2024 (476 of 1,897).

**Reading taken:** Year is the order-date year. Headline penalty is the first "$" amount in the recommendation, with the doubled "$" and space-after-comma rules applied. Denominators include $0 cases. Utility respondent uses the 13-name prefix test, ignoring case and leading spaces.

**Method and limits:** I could not run `cases.py`, because I have no shell tool here. Instead I counted rows in `reference/cases.csv` with regex searches anchored on the order date, respondent and first "$" amount. The raw counts were:

| All cases | 2,107 | 1,897 |
| Utility respondent | 605 | 476 |
| Non-utility respondent | 1,502 | 1,421 |
| Headline ≥ $5,000, all | 637 | 699 |
| Headline ≥ $5,000, utility | 15 | 12 |
| Headline ≥ $5,000, non-utility | 622 | 687 |

**Receipts:** I did not pull example rows, because the regex counts do not list rows.

**Caveats:**
- The regex assumes "$" appears only in the recommendation field before the abstentions and notes. I did not check this against every row.
