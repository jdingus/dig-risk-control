# H3

**Question:** Abstentions: a case has a **recorded abstention** if its Abstentions field has any text left after trimming spaces and deleting any stray words "Additional Infonnation:" or "Additional Information:" that leaked into it. What percentage of cases in each of 2021, 2024 and 2025 (by order year) have a recorded abstention? Round each to one decimal.

**Expected answer (from the committed key):** percent_with_abstention_2021 = 41.4; percent_with_abstention_2024 = 54.0; percent_with_abstention_2025 = 49.8

## How answers are scored

Each question has two or more required numbers. Per number: within tolerance = full, within the wider band
= half, otherwise zero. A question scores 2 (all numbers full), 1 (at least half the credit) or 0.
Percentages: full within ±0.5 points, half within ±2 points. Counts: full within ±1%, half within ±5%.
Dollar amounts: full if exact, half within ±10%. A named answer (a year, a company) must match exactly.
An answer labelled "estimate" is scored the same way: no credit for refusing, no penalty for saying so.

## Answer H3-01

**Results (by order year, cases with a recorded abstention):**

- **2021: 41.3%** (662 of 1,603 cases)
- **2024: 54.0%** (1,025 of 1,897 cases)
- **2025: 49.8%** (838 of 1,684 cases)

**How I got them:**
- I counted cases as "Case Number:" headings in each order's text file. Each case has exactly one Abstentions line, so the cases and Abstentions lines match (9,963 each across the 58 orders).
- A case has a recorded abstention when the text after "Abstentions:" is non-blank. No Abstentions field contains a leaked "Additional Information" string, so no adjustment was needed.
- Order years come from the date in each filename (for example, `ord_PSD_073124` is 2024-07-31), since the order text has no printed date. The filename-based split gives 11 orders in 2021, 12 in 2022, 14 in 2023, 11 in 2024, and 10 in 2025.

**Caveat:** I could not run a script because Bash is disabled in this session. The counts were done with Grep searches and checked file by file, but they were not computed by a program. The percentages depend on the filename dates being correct.
