I could not check this from the tables, so the figures may slightly understate the true share.
- The non-utility split uses the keyword heuristic in table N, but that heuristic only affects the breakdown within non-utility cases. The utility/non-utility split itself is the PREAMBLE prefix test, which matches your definition.
